## Description (407 chars)
ROADSUCTION is a 3D endless driving and flying game set in a neon city at night. Your gunmetal hover car has cold-gas thrusters on every side: blast off the road, flip over traffic, ride loops and stick to tunnel ceilings upside down. Shoot cartoon drones with 8 weapons, take down silly bosses every 2.5 km, grab credits and spend them on skins and upgrades while the weather and time of day keep changing.

## Instructions (326 chars)
Keyboard: hold SPACE to fly and hover, A/D or arrow keys to strafe, W/S to flip in the air, Q/E to barrel roll, SHIFT to boost, F or left-click to fire, 1-8 or X to switch weapons, P or ESC to pause. Touch: hold FLY, use the arrow buttons to strafe, tap FLIP, BOOST and FIRE. Land wheels-down and collect green orbs to repair.
