## Description (355 chars)
ROADSUCTION is a 3D endless driving and flying game set in a neon city at night. Your gunmetal hover hot-rod has cold-gas thrusters on every side: hit ramps, flip over traffic, ride loops and stick to tunnel ceilings upside down. Dodge googly-eyed space rocks, race the goofy rival TURBO NOODLE, foam cartoon drones with 8 weapons and knock out silly bosses. Daily missions, streaks and a top-10 of your longest runs.

## Instructions (326 chars)
Keyboard: hold SPACE to fly and hover, A/D or arrow keys to strafe, W/S to flip in the air, Q/E to barrel roll, SHIFT to boost, F or left-click to fire, 1-8 or X to switch weapons, P or ESC to pause. Touch: hold FLY, use the arrow buttons to strafe, tap FLIP, BOOST and FIRE. Land wheels-down and collect green orbs to repair.
